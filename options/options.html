Auto-enable sites

At each site listed here, the inspector starts by itself on every page you load. Chrome is asked for access to that one site only, and removing a site here hands that access straight back.

Privacy

  • The panel reads the page you are looking at, inside your browser.
  • Every setting on this page is stored locally in chrome.storage.local and is never transmitted.
  • Nothing leaves your browser except what you explicitly enable with Odoo Developer Mode: the clicked field's model and technical name, one fixed server setting used to detect your Odoo version, and — for an Odoo button or a view — its declaration, all sent to the same Odoo server you are already logged into.
  • ?debug=1 or ?debug=0 in a page's URL is read only in that page's own tab, is never stored, and cannot enable the inspector by itself.
